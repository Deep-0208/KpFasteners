## 5. FAQs (draft — 5 questions, 2-3 sentence answers)

**Q1: Do you supply direct to automotive OEMs?**
`[CLIENT TO CONFIRM — this is the top open item on this page]`. Our scope today covers tier-2 / tier-3 automotive component suppliers and heavy-engineering OEMs; direct passenger-car OEM supply (Tata / Mahindra / Maruti / Hyundai / M&HCV) typically requires IATF 16949 and PPAP Level 3, which we do not currently claim. For a specific OEM program, discuss the AVL and PPAP scope with us before you issue the RFQ.

**Q2: Can you supply PPAP documentation?**
PPAP Level 2 is available on request today `[CLIENT TO CONFIRM]`. Level 3 is reviewed case-by-case against the customer's specific requirement, line-audit window, and timing. All deliverables are built on top of our standard MTC EN 10204 3.1 + dimensional / hardness / coating-thickness in-house inspection chain, with NABL-partner tensile and PMI on request.

**Q3: What is the typical lead time for a drawing-based automotive fastener?**
10-21 days for an in-network recipe (standard PC 10.9 / 12.9 in common sizes with a standard coating), 4-8 weeks when a new die or tool is required, and 6-12 weeks for specialty materials or OEM-coded coatings (zinc-nickel / Dacromet / Geomet). Our MOQ default is 500 kg per SKU (`reference-defaults.md` row 17); below-MOQ feasibility is quoted per piece.

**Q4: Why do you avoid hot-dip galvanizing on PC 10.9 and 12.9?**
Hydrogen embrittlement risk per ISO 898-1 §9.6. Our default coating on PC 10.9 is **mechanical galvanized** or **zinc-nickel** (both avoid the aqueous hydrogen-introduction step); PC 12.9 is routinely supplied in **black oxide only**. HDG on 10.9 is offered only with a documented 190-230 °C bake-out protocol performed within 4 hours of plating. See `/materials/high-tensile-fasteners/` for the full HDE context.

**Q5: Can you supply A193 B7 / B16 flange studs for the power / oil & gas side?**
Yes — stud bolts to ASTM A193 B7 / B7M / B16 scope are one of our OEM lines; see `/products/stud-bolts/` for the catalogue. SS 316 / 316L studs (A193 B8M / B8M Class 2) for pressure-flange service are distributed from vetted partner mills with MTC pass-through. Each lot carries heat-number traceability and MTC EN 10204 3.1 by default.
