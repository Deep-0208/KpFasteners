## 5. FAQs (draft — 5 questions, 2-3 sentence answers)

**Q1: Which construction segments does KP actively serve?**
Pre-engineered buildings (PEB), high-rise RCC, bridges and flyovers, precast concrete yards, and tunnel / metro-rail lining fixings `[CLIENT TO CONFIRM metro / tunnel scope]`. The fastener BOM per project type is in §4.1; the material default per structure type is in §4.3. Our Ahmedabad plant is a practical fit for the Gujarat / Maharashtra / Rajasthan construction corridor with same-week dispatch within Gujarat.

**Q2: Do you supply fasteners against a structural engineer's drawing / project BOQ?**
Yes — send the structural drawing, the bolt schedule (shape, diameter, embedment / projection, coating, quantity per size) and the delivery pin code. We quote back against the drawing, with OEM foundation / anchor / stud / sag rods manufactured in-house and the balance (hex bolts, scaffold accessories, tie rods) sourced from vetted partner mills on the same PO.

**Q3: What is your typical lead time for a PEB foundation-bolt BOQ?**
Standard-shape anchor bolts (J-bolt / L-bolt / headed, M16-M30) in HDG dispatch 24-48 hours ex-Ahmedabad for Gujarat sites and 3-8 days pan-India. Custom-length or F1554 Gr 55 production runs 7-14 days. Headed F1554 Gr 105 heavy anchorage is on-quote with extended lead time (`reference-defaults.md` row 7).

**Q4: Can you supply fasteners for a bridge or metro-tunnel project?**
Yes for bridges — HDG 8.8 structural steel hex bolts, foundation studs for bearings and pedestals, parapet anchor bolts. For metro-tunnel lining fixings, we supply SS 316 fasteners (distribution range) and chemical anchor studs (OEM). Projects involving long-span bridge-stay cables or post-tensioning tendons are not in KP's product scope.

**Q5: What MTC and documentation do you provide on a construction BOQ?**
MTC EN 10204 3.1 is pass-through on distribution items and in-house on OEM lines. HDG coating-thickness per ISO 1461 is verified in-house with a gauge. Hardness is in-house; tensile and salt-spray are issued from a NABL-accredited partner lab on request. Batch traceability by heat number travels with the dispatch tag.
