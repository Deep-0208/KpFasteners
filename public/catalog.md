## 2. Fastener Material Grades & Mechanical Properties

### Carbon & Alloy Steel Property Classes (ISO 898-1):
- **Class 4.6:** Mild Steel (Nominal UTS: 400 MPa, Minimum Yield Strength: 240 MPa). Used for baseline civil foundation bolts and structural tie rods.
- **Class 8.8:** Medium Carbon Steel, Quenched & Tempered (UTS: 800 MPa min, Yield Strength: 640 MPa min). Standard high-tensile structural bolting.
- **Class 10.9:** Alloy Steel, Quenched & Tempered (UTS: 1,040 MPa min, Yield Strength: 940 MPa min). Heavy engineering, vibrating machinery, and structural baseplates.
- **Class 12.9:** High-Alloy Tool Steel (UTS: 1,220 MPa min, Yield Strength: 1,100 MPa min). Socket head cap screws, dies, and tooling fixtures.

### Petrochemical & High-Temperature Stud Alloys (ASTM A193 / A194):
- **ASTM A193 Grade B7:** Chromium-Molybdenum (AISI 4140/4142), quenched & tempered. Minimum tensile 125 ksi (860 MPa), yield 105 ksi (720 MPa). Service temperature -29 °C to +427 °C. Mating nut: ASTM A194 Grade 2H.
- **ASTM A193 Grade B8:** AISI 304 Stainless Steel, solution annealed. Class 1 tensile 75 ksi (515 MPa), yield 30 ksi (205 MPa). Mating nut: ASTM A194 Grade 8.
- **ASTM A193 Grade B8M:** AISI 316 Stainless Steel (molybdenum bearing). High-chloride, marine, and corrosive chemical service. Mating nut: ASTM A194 Grade 8M.

### Stainless Steel Grades (ISO 3506-1):
- **Grade A2-70 (AISI 304):** Tensile strength 700 MPa min, yield strength 450 MPa min. General industrial anti-corrosive fasteners.
- **Grade A4-70 / A4-80 (AISI 316):** Tensile strength 700-800 MPa min, yield strength 450-600 MPa min. Marine, coastal, effluent plant, and chemical exposure.

## 3. Surface Coatings & Corrosion Resistance Specifications

1. **Hot-Dip Galvanizing (HDG):** ASTM A153 Class C / IS 2629. Minimum average coating thickness 85 µm (minimum coating mass 610 g/m²). Standard outdoor civil anchorage coating.
2. **Zinc Electroplating:** IS 1573 / ASTM B633. Trivalent blue, yellow, or clear passivation. 5-15 µm thickness.
3. **PTFE / Fluoropolymer Coating:** Xylan 1424 / Fluorokote 1. Applied over zinc phosphating. Salt spray resistance > 1,500 hours to ASTM B117; low friction coefficient for repeat flange makeup.
4. **Hydrogen Embrittlement Safeguard:** Class 10.9 and high-strength fasteners undergo a mandatory 190-230 °C hydrogen de-embrittlement bake-out for ≥ 4 hours within 4 hours of electroplating.
